## Answers

### 1. Domains

tenderfern.github.io and www.youtube-nocookie.com, for the privacy-enhanced API mode and the plain iframe. On iPadOS
17.7, Only Approved Websites blocked the player frames from unlisted hosts. It did not block the API script, the video
data or the ads, which all come from hosts that were not on the list.

### 2. www.youtube.com

Not needed. The privacy-enhanced API mode and the plain iframe both play with www.youtube.com unlisted. The API
script is fetched from www.youtube.com and loaded anyway. The default-host API mode needs it, because its frame is
www.youtube.com; that was not tested.

### 3. Ads

Pre-roll ads play under the smallest list, on a made-for-kids video too, and Skip works. The list cannot stop them:
the ad requests go to hosts that are not on it. Mid-rolls were not tested.

### 4. End of a video

With rel=0, in both privacy-enhanced modes: a replay button, the title and channel with the channel icon, More
videos (a row of videos from the same channel, with a close button), the YouTube logo and a link icon. No grid of
suggested videos. The API sends onStateChange 0 (ended). Whether creator end-screen elements appeared in The Egg's
last 20 s was not recorded. Fullscreen was not checked.

### 5. Ways out

With the YouTube app installed, a tap on a More videos tile, on the title, or on the YouTube logo opens the app and
plays there. The page receives nothing: the player state stays ended (0) and the video id does not change. Not
tested: these taps with the app removed, the channel icon, the link icon.

### 6. Signals

Seen: onReady, onStateChange -1, 3, 1 and 0, onApiChange, onPlaybackQualityChange. During a pre-roll ad the state
stays -1 (unstarted), onApiChange fires as the ad starts and ends, and getVideoUrl keeps the video's own id. So a
check for no playing state within 12 s of the play tap fires during an ad: both candidate sign-in lines in this log
(14:48:34.9 and 15:56:34.0) were ads. No onError was seen, because the error cases were not run. Leaving for the
YouTube app produced no event.
